Star Swarm — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Publisher: arrowed · Android package: io.arrowed.starswarm
The short version: Star Swarm is a single-player arcade game. No accounts, and your progress lives only on your device. If you are 13 or older, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can improve it (see Gameplay statistics below) — never your name, email, contacts, location or advertising ID. The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, and Google may process your advertising ID and device data to serve them (see Advertising below). We do not sell personal data.

1. Information we collect

Star Swarm has no servers of its own and never asks for your name, email, contacts or location. Two Google services process data on our behalf: Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous gameplay statistics, players 13+ only) and Google AdMob (advertising), both described below. We do not sell personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your device only — unlocked ships, upgrade levels, in-game currency, settings (camera, controls, sound, haptics), and daily-reward streaks. This save data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not accessible to us or anyone else;
  • is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Notifications

Star Swarm can show local notifications (for example, a reminder that your daily reward is ready). These are scheduled entirely on your device — no server is involved and no data is sent anywhere. The game asks for notification permission before enabling them, and you can turn them off at any time in your device's system settings.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) — used only for the local reminders described above.
  • Vibration — used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay; can be disabled in the in-game Settings.
  • Internet & advertising ID — internet is used for ads and anonymous gameplay statistics; the advertising ID is used only by Google AdMob to load and measure ads (see Advertising), never by analytics.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

5. In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases are governed by Google Play's terms and privacy policy. Purchase entitlements are stored locally on your device.

6. Third-party services

Star Swarm is built with the Unity engine and distributed through Google Play. These platforms may process limited, non-identifying device information (such as device model or crash diagnostics) as part of operating their services, under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not receive personal data from either of them.

Advertising

Star Swarm shows optional rewarded ads — an ad plays only when you choose one (for example REVIVE, 2× SCRAP or a reroll). Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device information and how you interact with the ads, under Google's privacy policy (see also how Google uses data from partner apps).

  • Your age: on first launch the game asks your age range (under 13 / 13–15 / 16+). This answer is stored only on your device and is used solely to choose how ads are served: under-13 players get child-directed, non-personalised, family-safe ads; 13–15-year-olds get non-personalised ads.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ads are requested, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & Terms → AD PRIVACY CHOICES.
  • Your controls: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.

We (the developer) do not receive your advertising ID or any personal data from AdMob — only aggregated, anonymous ad reports.

Gameplay statistics

If you tell the age screen you are 13 or older, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase privacy). Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing is ever sent for players under 13.

  • What is sent: game events — a run started or ended (ship, time survived, level, kills, scrap earned), which upgrade card you picked, tutorial progress, ships unlocked, in-game currency spent, and whether you chose to watch a rewarded ad. Firebase also records basic app and device information (app version, device model, OS version, country derived from the IP address, session length) and a random app-instance ID that is reset when you uninstall the game.
  • What is NOT sent: your name, email, contacts, precise location, or advertising ID. Analytics data is never used for advertising — ad-related collection and ad personalisation are switched off in the analytics SDK.
  • Why: to understand which parts of the game players enjoy or quit at, and to balance difficulty and rewards.
  • Retention: Google keeps event-level data for at most 14 months, then deletes it automatically.
  • Your controls: uninstalling the game resets the app-instance ID. You can also contact us to ask about this data.

7. Children

Star Swarm is suitable for a general audience. We collect no personal data from anyone, including children; players under 13 send no gameplay statistics at all. Players who tell the in-game age screen they are under 13 only receive child-directed, non-personalised, family-safe ads (AdMob's child-directed treatment), and players under 16 receive non-personalised ads.

8. Data security and retention

We run no servers of our own. Anonymous gameplay statistics are stored by Google Analytics for Firebase under Google's security practices and deleted within 14 months. Your save data is protected by your device's own security and is removed when you uninstall the game.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game ever adds features that change how data is handled (for example, ads, cloud saves, or online features), this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the updated policy will be available at the same location as this one and inside the game.
