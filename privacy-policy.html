Star Swarm — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Publisher: arrowed · Android package: io.arrowed.starswarm
The short version: Star Swarm is an offline, single-player arcade game. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything the game saves lives on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

1. Information we collect

None. Star Swarm does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The game does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, or any other personal information, and does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that profile you.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your device only — unlocked ships, upgrade levels, in-game currency, settings (camera, controls, sound, haptics), and daily-reward streaks. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not accessible to us or anyone else;
  • is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Notifications

Star Swarm can show local notifications (for example, a reminder that your daily reward is ready). These are scheduled entirely on your device — no server is involved and no data is sent anywhere. The game asks for notification permission before enabling them, and you can turn them off at any time in your device's system settings.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) — used only for the local reminders described above.
  • Vibration — used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay; can be disabled in the in-game Settings.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

5. In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases are governed by Google Play's terms and privacy policy. Purchase entitlements are stored locally on your device.

6. Third-party services

Star Swarm is built with the Unity engine and distributed through Google Play. These platforms may process limited, non-identifying device information (such as device model or crash diagnostics) as part of operating their services, under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not receive personal data from either of them.

7. Children

Star Swarm is suitable for a general audience and collects no data from anyone, including children. Because no personal information is collected at all, no information is collected from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Data security and retention

Since no data is transmitted or stored by us, there is nothing for us to secure or retain on a server. Your save data is protected by your device's own security and is removed when you uninstall the game.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game ever adds features that change how data is handled (for example, ads, cloud saves, or online features), this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the updated policy will be available at the same location as this one and inside the game.
